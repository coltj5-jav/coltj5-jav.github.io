Empowering people can be dangerous. 

Once empowered, they realize the constraints they've been operating under. 

This undoubtedly comes with cognitive dissonance, and a compel to fight the resistance. 

Despite being the one who freed them from that bondage, they look to offload it -- as a reduction in pain. 

Unfortunately, the person in front of them can be **you**.

They offload that pain, projecting it towards you. 

So the first person they will appear to "set a boundary" with is you. 

While that's progress, the anger is misplaced. 

Its quite the phenomenon. Tread lightly.
