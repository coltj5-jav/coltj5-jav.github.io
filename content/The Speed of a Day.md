It is challenging to remember that one day moves at the speed of *one day*.

It is a choice to experience that passing day at whichever speed we want. 

- Passing by quickly? Choose to slow it down. 
- Passing by slowly? *Start making things happen*. 
- Moments of bliss? Enjoy the moment and let it happen.

Just arrive at the moment with yourself.
