It is challenging to remember that one day moves at the speed of *one day*.

It is a choice to experience that passing day at whatever speed that we want to choose. 

If it’s happening faster, then what we want, then we can choose to slow it down. 
If it’s happening slower, then what we want then we can choose to*start making things happen*. 

If it’s passing by with moments of bliss -- then just enjoy the moment and let it happen.

Just arrive at the moment with yourself.
