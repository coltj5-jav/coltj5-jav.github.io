The best thing about life is that you can honestly give yourself a day to do whatever you want. Life is not going to crumble. 

- Flip through the course or book 
- Scheme out that product idea
- Call your parents, grow with them
- Relax in the sun & breathe a bit

All of these will bring you back to yourself, so you stop running away from the task at hand.
