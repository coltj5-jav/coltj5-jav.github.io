There is a real cost to leadership styles.

Two styles I recently encountered:

A: leading team members to be autonomous, empowered.
B: only sharing what is necessary, keeping a frame.

One may start off slow at first.
The other does micro-executions, rapidly.

What happens when the leader is absent, conditions change, & they abandon the relationship?

The objective ceases to be completed.

Train your people to be empowered, so that they eventually become autonomous.

Leadership is unlocking the creativity & potential is someone.
Not keeping them dependent upon you.
