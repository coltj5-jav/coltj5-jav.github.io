
_A Course in Miracles_ (ACIM) is a unique spiritual self-study program designed to shift our perception of the world from fear to love.

It was channeled by Dr. Helen Schucman in the '60s & '70s. 

Geared towards the *Western mind*, you'll notice that it speaks to/through the *Christian* lens, as its intended audience has Christianity as the fundamental operating system installed.

Its principles are a powerful framework for one's initiation to remove oneself from outdated power matrices, so that one can establish their own connection with God, our Creator.

While growing up in a small town, Catholic community -- my family practiced good ethics & principles, but did not cling very hard to concrete religion.

Through my 20s, I began exploring other philosophical systems -- seeking answers to questions that I could not find answers for.

Undoubtedly, one in this quest will be "ushered" back into a religious belief system as "salvation".

Watching many friends become what I deem as *born-again Christians*, I observed temporary relief arriving to them -- albeit the inner discovery process had also been paused.

Ironically, this is antithetical to the very teachings of the person they worship.

Stumbling across *A Course in Miracles* radically changed my "lens", creating a happier, healthier life & connection with God -- which can be cultivated every single waking moment of the day *(no religion required).*

I typically cover one principle per day as a review.
This will be my 2nd time going through the course.
