I've been mapping out the business matrix for most of my adulthood (>age 20). Across that journey, I've met many others who are forming their own opinions about business, what exists beyond this experience, etc.

There's a funny quote I've come across:

> *The businessman is far more spiritual than the sedated hippie, utilizing mind, body, and spirit for the service of others.*
> 
> *The sedated hippie, in his selfishness & lethargy, is only concerned with feeling good, while the businessman is concerned with doing good.*


As a younger lad, I came across many that were still moving through traumas & paradigms -- so anything metaphysical was used to "help me make more money." That was about the extent of that exploration.

In a fortunate sequential series of events, I was introduced to the *spiritual side of life* -- which I will explain later.

Traveling that path, I also met people who poo-poo'd on business. They typically masked their insecurity & bypassed their own inner work with spiritual Ego.

I'm tickled to have come across some of the content in the spiritual, metaphysical that I have.

It helped me break from the Religion matrix, so that I could develop an authentic connection with God.

Studying business + metaphysical aspects of life, I've arrived at a healthy relationship with the financial/business matrix <> deeper meanings & alternative quests of life.

One thing is for certain: **businesses are amazing vehicles to the expansion of human potential.**

The unfortunate aspect of our collective matrix is that this fact gets hidden from people, and sometimes abused.
