I have a peculiar thing that happens to me.

As I travel the world with my partner, its become even more apparent that it happens to *me*, and not necessarily *everyone*. 

(I'm sure I'm not the only one -- but you get the point).

My partner will end up meeting lots of different archetypes & people, all of which we end up processing the meaning of later together.

For myself -- anytime I enter into the public space, I am always met with ONE person.

I've expressed that I don't want to "fast" travel as much, as we're busy building businesses.

This means staying in one place for at least 90 days or more.

With some consistency in my physical location, one would think this would mean that I could potentially meet "more" people.

Yes, I meet people here & there with the common *"Hello!"* *"How are you?"*
I'm talking about a deeper conversation.
One where you actually get to know someone.
