
#### Core Idea: 
	Empty your mind of pre-assigned meanings.

This is the first step to seeing things differently, with eyes of love – instead of judgement or fear. The meanings we assign to objects and experiences are not inherent, but projected by our minds. 

By recognizing that nothing we see has inherent meaning, we begin to loosen our attachment to preconceived judgments and interpretations.

It's the first step in rewiring our brain.

#### What Its Teaching Us
We think we understand what things are: a chair, a phone, a person’s face -- but the Course is guiding us to see that all these meanings are based on past associations, not truth. 

By admitting that we don’t know what things mean, we create space for a deeper, more loving perception to come through.

It’s a clean slate. A reset of our inner vision.

ACIM is mind training to change your default thoughts, beliefs, and actions --  ultimately leading to joy, peace, and safety.

This creates space for new perceptions and prepares us for later lessons that will help us discover true meaning beyond the physical realm of perception.

#### How to Apply It Today
A common mistake is trying to understand the material intellectually, rather than practicing it; joy, peace, and understanding come as a package through practice.

Look around you slowly. Apply the idea to everything you see, without excluding anything.

Do this for a minute or two, a few times throughout the day.

#### Food for Thought
- What happens when I stop assigning meaning to everything around me?
- What feelings or resistance come up?
