
#### Core Idea 
	Let go of the illusion of understanding. It is okay to admit that we don’t truly understand any of it.

#### What Its Teaching Us
The Course is helping you loosen your grip on the ego's version of “knowledge.”	

It builds on the idea that we’ve given things meaning... but now goes deeper by asking us to admit: **we don’t truly understand any of it.**

We walk around assuming we know what things are, what they mean, how they work... but much of that “knowing” is rooted in conditioning, labels, assumptions, and past experiences.

Our interpretations are based on past experiences and judgments rather than true understanding.

**This lesson invites you to pause and say**: *What if I don’t actually understand anything I see? What if I’ve just made up meanings based on a limited view?*

Acknowledging "I do not understand" opens the mind to new possibilities beyond our current limited perceptions. It helps break down intellectual arrogance and the false certainty that blocks spiritual insight.

**Recognizing our lack of understanding creates space for true knowledge to enter our awareness.**

It’s not asking to you feel lost or confused. 
It’s freeing you from false certainty, so you can open to true vision.

This lesson prepares us to receive a new vision by first acknowledging the limitations of our current sight.

#### How to Apply It Today
As before, look around the space you’re in. Let your eyes rest briefly on each thing, and say:

> *“I do not understand this [object].”*
>*“I do not understand that [person/thing/scene].”*

Use it on both neutral and emotionally charged things. It helps to include both.

Notice how often your mind wants to explain or categorize. Just gently return to the practice without fighting those thoughts.

#### Food for Thought
- What’s one situation I think I fully “understand” right now? 
- What would it feel like to admit that I might not see the full picture? 
- Could there be more to it than I realize?
