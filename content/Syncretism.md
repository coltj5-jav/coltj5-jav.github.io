**Syncretism** ([/ˈsɪŋkrətɪzəm/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English"))[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syncretism#cite_note-1) is the combining or merging of various distinct beliefs or [schools of thought](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_thought "School of thought").
